1 · AGE
Under-30s default at about 11.5%, more than double the over-60s.
2 · GENDER & JOB
Males default at 10.17% vs 7.01% for females (about 1.5x), and higher in every age band. Low-skill labourers lead at 17.2%.
3 · THE SURPRISE
Credit-to-income groups sit within 1.2 points of each other, and "High Risk" defaults least (7.25%).
Source: Home Credit dataset, 305,545 applicants, analysed in MySQL and Power BI. Default rate = defaulters ÷ applicants. Credit-to-income = loan amount ÷ annual income (Low < 3, Moderate 3–6, High > 6). Excludes 4 records with unknown gender.
